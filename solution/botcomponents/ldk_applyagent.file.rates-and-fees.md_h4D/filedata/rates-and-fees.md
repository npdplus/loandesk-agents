# SME Working Capital Loan — Rates and Fees

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

Effective from **1 November 2026** until replaced.

## Interest rates

Fixed rate per year for the whole term. Interest is calculated on the reducing balance.

| Loan amount (THB) | 6 to 12 months | 18 to 24 months | 36 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100,000 to 1,000,000 | 7.50 % | 7.75 % | 8.00 % |
| 1,000,001 to 3,000,000 | 7.00 % | 7.25 % | 7.50 % |
| 3,000,001 to 5,000,000 | 6.50 % | 6.75 % | 7.00 % |

Example: THB 2,000,000 over 24 months has a rate of **7.25 %** a year.

## Fees

| Fee | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee | None | — |
| Front-end fee | 1.00 % of the loan amount, minimum THB 2,000 | Deducted when the loan is paid out |
| Early repayment fee | 2.00 % of the amount repaid early | Only if repaid within the first 12 months |
| Late payment interest | Contract rate + 3.00 % a year on the overdue amount | From the first day overdue |
| Statement copy | THB 100 per copy | On request |

## Notes

- The rate shown to you before you sign is the rate for your whole term.
- The rate depends only on the amount and the term. The LoanDesk assistant cannot offer a different rate.
- Rates may change for new loans. They never change for a loan already signed.
