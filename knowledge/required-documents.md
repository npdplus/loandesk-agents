# SME Working Capital Loan — Required Documents

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

Send clear copies (PDF or photo). We start the 5-business-day review only when we have every document on the
list for your business type.

## All businesses

- [ ] Completed application (submitted through the LoanDesk assistant)
- [ ] Bank statements of the business account for the **last 6 months**
- [ ] Copy of the guarantor's Thai national ID card, or passport and work permit
- [ ] A short description of how the loan will be used

## Limited company

- [ ] Company registration certificate issued within the **last 3 months**
- [ ] List of shareholders (form BOJ.5) issued within the last 3 months
- [ ] Audited financial statements for the **last 2 years**
- [ ] VAT registration certificate (form PP.20), if the company is VAT-registered
- [ ] Board resolution approving the loan, if the articles require one

## Registered partnership

- [ ] Partnership registration certificate issued within the **last 3 months**
- [ ] Financial statements for the **last 2 years**
- [ ] VAT registration certificate (form PP.20), if registered

## Sole proprietor

- [ ] Commercial registration certificate
- [ ] Personal income tax returns (form PND.90) for the **last 2 years**
- [ ] Copy of the owner's house registration

## Notes

- Documents in a language other than Thai or English need a certified translation.
- We may ask for more documents if they are needed to decide. We tell you which ones and why.
- Do not send documents by e-mail. Upload them through the LoanDesk assistant when asked.
