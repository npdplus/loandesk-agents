# SME Working Capital Loan — Product Sheet

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

## What it is

A term loan that helps a small or medium-sized business pay for day-to-day needs: stock, payroll, supplier
bills and small equipment. The business repays the same amount every month.

## Key facts

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Loan amount | THB 100,000 to THB 5,000,000 |
| Maximum amount for your business | Up to 30 % of your annual revenue, and never more than THB 5,000,000 |
| Term | 6, 12, 18, 24 or 36 months |
| Repayment | Equal monthly instalments of principal and interest |
| Interest rate | Fixed for the whole term, from 6.50 % to 8.00 % a year, depending on amount and term (see *Rates and Fees*) |
| Collateral | Not required for this product |
| Guarantor | The business owner or a director guarantees the loan personally |
| Decision time | Within 5 business days after we receive all required documents |

## Who can apply

Businesses registered in Thailand that have operated for at least 2 years and have an annual revenue of at
least THB 1,200,000. The full rules are in the *Eligibility Policy*.

## Allowed uses

- Buying stock or raw materials
- Paying salaries and supplier invoices
- Buying small equipment (each item up to THB 500,000)

## Not allowed

- Buying land or buildings
- Repaying loans at other banks
- Personal spending by owners or directors
- Speculative investments, including shares, gold and cryptocurrency

## How to apply

1. Check your eligibility with the LoanDesk assistant or on this page.
2. Prepare the documents in the *Required Documents* checklist.
3. Submit the application through the LoanDesk assistant.
4. A credit officer reviews the application and decides within 5 business days.
5. If approved, sign the loan contract at a Contoso Bank branch.

The LoanDesk assistant is an AI assistant. It answers questions and pre-checks applications, but **a credit
officer makes every loan decision**. See the *Privacy and AI Notice*.
