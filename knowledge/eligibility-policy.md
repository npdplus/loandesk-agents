# SME Working Capital Loan — Eligibility Policy

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

A business must meet **all** of the rules below to apply. Meeting the rules does not guarantee approval: a
credit officer reviews every application.

## Rules for the business

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| E1 | Registered in Thailand as a limited company, a registered partnership, or a sole proprietor with a commercial registration |
| E2 | Has operated for **at least 2 years** since registration |
| E3 | Annual revenue of **at least THB 1,200,000** in the latest full year |
| E4 | No loan or credit card payment overdue by more than 30 days at any lender in the last 12 months |
| E5 | Not in bankruptcy or business rehabilitation proceedings |
| E6 | The business is not in an excluded industry (see below) |

## Rules for the request

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Amount between **THB 100,000 and THB 5,000,000** |
| R2 | Amount no more than **30 % of annual revenue** |
| R3 | Term of 6, 12, 18, 24 or 36 months |
| R4 | The purpose is an allowed use (see the *Product Sheet*) |

## Rules for the guarantor

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| G1 | The owner or a director, aged **20 to 65** on the application date |
| G2 | Holds a Thai national ID card or a valid passport with a work permit |

## Excluded industries

Gambling, weapons, adult entertainment, cryptocurrency trading and money lending.

## Examples

- A company with 3 years in business and THB 2,000,000 revenue asks for THB 500,000 over 12 months.
  It meets E2, E3, R1, R2 (THB 500,000 is 25 % of revenue) and R3, so it **can apply** if the other rules are
  also met.
- A shop with 1 year in business asks for THB 200,000. It fails E2 and **cannot apply yet**.
- A business with THB 1,500,000 revenue asks for THB 600,000. That is 40 % of revenue and fails R2. It can
  apply for up to **THB 450,000**.

## Pre-check by the LoanDesk assistant

The assistant checks E2, E3, R1, R2 and R3 from the answers you give. The result is one of:

- **Eligible to apply:** the answers meet these rules. A credit officer still decides.
- **Not eligible:** at least one rule is not met. The assistant says which rule.
- **Needs review:** the assistant cannot tell from your answers. A credit officer will check.

The pre-check is not a loan decision.
