# LoanDesk — Privacy and AI Notice

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

## You are talking to an AI assistant

The LoanDesk assistant is an artificial intelligence (AI) system. It can:

- answer questions about the SME Working Capital Loan from Contoso Bank's published documents;
- pre-check whether your business can apply;
- collect your application and documents and summarise them for a credit officer;
- tell you the status of your application.

It cannot:

- approve, reject or promise a loan;
- change a rate or a fee;
- give investment, tax or legal advice.

**A credit officer makes every loan decision** and records the reason. If your application is declined, you
can ask for the reason and for a review by another officer.

## Talking to a person

You can ask for a person at any time by typing "talk to a person". A member of the Lending Centre replies
within 1 business day, Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:00.

Contoso Bank Lending Centre (fictional): lending@contoso-bank.example

## How we use your information

| What | Why | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Your questions to the assistant | To answer you and to improve answers | 90 days |
| Application details and documents | To assess your application | 10 years after the loan ends, or 2 years if no loan is made |
| The assistant's summary of your application | To help the credit officer; it is marked as AI-generated | Same as the application |

We do not use your information to train AI models. We do not sell it. We share it only with the credit
bureau for the credit check, and as the law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to its use, by writing to the Lending
Centre. Some information must be kept by law.
