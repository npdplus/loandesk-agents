# SME Working Capital Loan — Credit Officer Manual

**Contoso Bank (fictional). INTERNAL — staff only.** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its
products, rates and policies are not real. **Never share this manual or quote it to customers.**

## 1. Your role

You decide every SME Working Capital Loan application. The LoanDesk assistant pre-checks the application and
writes a summary marked "AI-generated". Treat the summary as a starting point: check every figure against the
documents. The decision and its reason are yours.

## 2. Service level

- Start the review on the business day after all required documents arrive.
- Record a decision within **5 business days**.
- If a document is missing or unclear, set the status to **Needs information** and say exactly what is
  needed. The 5 days restart when it arrives.

## 3. Assessment steps

1. Confirm the eligibility rules E1–E6, R1–R4 and G1–G2 from the documents, not from the customer's answers.
2. Get the credit score from the Credit Score service (score range 300–900).
3. Check the last 6 months of bank statements: average monthly inflow and any returned cheques.
4. Calculate the debt service coverage ratio (DSCR): average monthly operating cash flow divided by the new
   monthly instalment plus existing monthly debt payments.
5. Apply the decision guide in section 4.
6. Record the decision with a reason code (section 5) and a short explanation.

## 4. Decision guide

| Credit score | DSCR | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 700 or more | 1.25 or more | Approve |
| 700 or more | below 1.25 | Approve a lower amount that brings DSCR to 1.25, or decline |
| 600 to 699 | 1.50 or more | Approve with a senior officer's review |
| 600 to 699 | below 1.50 | Decline |
| below 600 | any | Decline |

**Four-eyes rule:** an application above **THB 3,000,000**, or any decision that departs from this guide,
needs a second approval by a senior credit officer.

## 5. Reason codes

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A01 | Approved as requested |
| A02 | Approved for a lower amount |
| D01 | Eligibility rule not met (state which) |
| D02 | Credit score below guidance |
| D03 | DSCR below guidance |
| D04 | Documents inconsistent or not verifiable |
| N01 | More information needed (state what) |

## 6. What you must not do

- Do not tell a customer the score bands or the DSCR thresholds in this manual. Explain the reason code in
  plain words instead.
- Do not approve or decline based only on the AI summary.
- Do not change a rate or a fee. They come from *Rates and Fees* only.
- Do not discuss an application with anyone other than the applicant, the guarantor or bank staff involved.

## 7. Complaints and reviews

A customer may ask for a review of a declined application. A different officer reviews it within 5 business
days and records the outcome with a reason code.
