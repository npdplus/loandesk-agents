# SME Working Capital Loan — Frequently Asked Questions

**Contoso Bank (fictional).** This document is part of a demo. Contoso Bank, its products, rates and policies
are not real.

## About the loan

**How much can I borrow?**
From THB 100,000 to THB 5,000,000, and no more than 30 % of your business's annual revenue.

**How long can I borrow for?**
6, 12, 18, 24 or 36 months.

**What is the interest rate?**
A fixed rate from 6.50 % to 8.00 % a year. It depends on the amount and the term; see *Rates and Fees*.

**Do I need collateral?**
No. The owner or a director guarantees the loan personally.

**What can I use the money for?**
Stock, raw materials, salaries, supplier invoices and small equipment up to THB 500,000 per item. Not land,
buildings, repaying other loans, personal spending or speculative investments.

**Are there any fees?**
No application fee. A front-end fee of 1.00 % (minimum THB 2,000) is deducted when the loan is paid out. Early
repayment within the first 12 months costs 2.00 % of the amount repaid early.

## Applying

**Who can apply?**
Businesses registered in Thailand, in business for at least 2 years, with annual revenue of at least
THB 1,200,000 and no payments overdue by more than 30 days in the last 12 months.

**My business is 18 months old. Can I apply?**
Not yet. The business must have operated for at least 2 years.

**Can a sole proprietor apply?**
Yes, with a commercial registration certificate and the documents for sole proprietors.

**What documents do I need?**
It depends on your business type; see *Required Documents*. Every business needs 6 months of bank statements.

**How long does a decision take?**
Within 5 business days after we have all the required documents.

**Can the assistant approve my loan?**
No. The assistant only pre-checks and prepares your application. A credit officer decides.

**Does passing the pre-check mean I will be approved?**
No. It means you can apply. The credit officer also checks your credit history and finances.

## After applying

**How do I check my application status?**
Ask the LoanDesk assistant with your application number, which starts with "LDK-".

**My application was declined. What can I do?**
Ask for the reason and for a review by another officer. You can apply again after 3 months.

**Can I repay early?**
Yes. Within the first 12 months there is a 2.00 % fee on the amount repaid early. After 12 months there is no
fee.

**What if I pay late?**
Late payment interest is your contract rate plus 3.00 % a year on the overdue amount, from the first day.

## Talking to a person

**How do I reach a person?**
Type "talk to a person" in the assistant, or write to lending@contoso-bank.example (fictional). We reply within
1 business day.
